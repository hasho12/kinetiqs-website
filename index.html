THE LOCAL-FIRST PATH

Less distance.
More ownership.

Keep everyday records close. Export when you choose. Use optional backup when it makes sense for you.

Core records stay on the device in this illustration.

THE DESIGN CHOICECloud-dependent approachThe local-first approach ↗
Everyday accessCan depend on connectivityCore functions available offline
Primary storageA remote serviceYour device
PortabilityDepends on provider export toolsDesigned around user-controlled export
RecoveryDepends on the providerRequires your own export or backup

A comparison of design approaches, not every app. Local storage still needs device security and a backup plan. Sign-in, purchases and optional connected features may require the internet.
