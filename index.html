04 / OUR PHILOSOPHY

CLOSE BY ARCHITECTURE.
↳

Great technology.
Less between
you and your life.

Local-first is a starting point. Your device holds the everyday. Connected services have a purpose. And your data should have a way out.

Ask us about our approach ↗
01

Start close.

Design core experiences around the device in your hands, with less dependence on a connection.

02

Make it useful.

Bring clarity to everyday routines. Thoughtful details, considered features, and room to breathe.

03

Leave the door open.

Design around user-controlled export and optional backup. Ownership includes the ability to leave.

A SMALLER DISTANCE

Your world.
Closer to home.

Core records stay on the device in this illustration.

Local storage still needs device security and a backup plan. Sign-in, purchases and optional connected features may require the internet. Individual app policies will detail their behaviour at launch.

Two approaches. Different trade-offs.+
Cloud-dependent and local-first design approaches, not an audit of individual apps
The design choiceCloud-dependent approachLocal-first approach
Everyday accessCan depend on connectivityCore functions available offline
Primary storageA remote serviceYour device
PortabilityDepends on provider export toolsDesigned for user-controlled export
RecoveryDepends on the providerRequires your own export or backup
